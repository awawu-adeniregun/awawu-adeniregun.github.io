Featured project

HR Analytics Dashboard

Independent HR analytics projectMicrosoft Power BIJanuary – October 2026Synthetic workforce data

This project is a multi-page HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI. It shows how workforce data can become meaningful insight for HR planning, monitoring and management decision-making.

The dashboard brings together key HR metrics across the employee lifecycle: workforce composition, recruitment, retention, compensation, attendance, performance and workforce risk.

The aim was a central reporting tool that makes HR information easier to monitor, analyse and interpret. It demonstrates how I organise HR data, develop workforce indicators, build interactive reports and present people information for management review.

    Dashboard highlights

    How HR data translates into management insight. All figures are from the synthetic dataset.

    Performance Management

    Q1 to Q3. Q4 appraisals were not yet due.

    • 153 employees appraised
    • 76.5 average performance score
    • 54 top performers
    • 61 solid performers
    • 38 low performers
    • 30 employees on Performance Improvement Plans
    • 70 employees improved
    • 64 employees declined

    Insight: identifies high performers, people who need support, and where targeted performance action may be needed.

    Disciplinary Management

    January to October 2026

    • 40 cases recorded
    • 35 employees disciplined
    • 95% case closure rate
    • 2 open cases
    • 5 repeat offenders

    Insight: gives visibility of conduct, recurring issues, case resolution and areas that need management attention.

    Workforce Risk & Succession

    As at the end of October 2026

    • 172 active employees
    • 65 critical roles identified
    • 36 of them rated critical for succession
    • 2 critical roles held by people at or within 2 years of retirement age

    Insight: shows which roles need succession planning, knowledge transfer and proactive talent management.

    Overall, the dashboard shows how HR can move beyond routine reporting and give management structured information for workforce planning, performance monitoring, employee relations and organisational risk management.
